# Changelog

## 0.8

- New source (Atlanta Hobby) checked against the hover, harrier and torque roll: more instructions are now backed by additional sources.
- Hover: the sources disagree on where the pull-up starts and on how to change the throttle (both shown as notes); leaving the hover is "add throttle".
- Fly RC articles re-read: the knife edge rudder rule gets one more source, the flat spin no longer cites one article for holding the elevator and rudder, and the throttle note of the flat spin exit says that the source speaks on both sides.
- Torque roll: that the elevator and rudder corrections change direction as the model turns is now confirmed by two sources and shown as an instruction.

## 0.7

- The small previews of the motor glider figures show smaller models, so they no longer overlap.

## 0.6

- New category "Motor glider" with four figures: loop, split S, roll and stall turn, each with its sources, a notice that motor gliders differ a lot, and German and English texts.

## 0.5

- The language you choose is remembered in your browser and applies on every page.
- On first visit to the English start page, a German browser is taken to the German version.

## 0.4

- Links shared in messengers and social networks now show a large preview image with the logo, in English and German.
- Pages tell search engines their canonical address and their other language (hreflang); sitemap, robots file, web app manifest and home-screen icons added.
- New FAQ page in English (`/faq`) and German (`/de/faq`), linked in the footer.

## 0.3

- The settings gear now has the same shape as in TLMscope.

## 0.2

- Settings (language, colour scheme, stick mode) moved into a settings dialog; the header only shows a gear icon. The stick mode label at the transmitter opens it too.
- Imprint and privacy policy are now separate pages: `/legal` (English) and `/de/legal` (German).
- A thin progress bar at the top of the main view shows that the animation is running.

## 0.1

- First version: seven figures (knife edge, elevator, harrier, hover, torque roll, rolling harrier, flat spin) with a 3D model and both transmitter sticks moving together in slow motion.
- Manoeuvre browser with the categories beginner, advanced and pro; each figure card shows a small version of the figure at a glance.
- Every instruction names its sources (at least two independent ones); disputed and missing parts are shown as such.
- Control surfaces glow yellow-orange in proportion to their deflection, on the model, in the control schematic and in the flight-path overview.
- Optional motor sound that follows the throttle (off by default).
- Mode 1 / Mode 2 switch, playback speeds, phase buttons and a clickable figure strip.
- English and German, light and dark theme.
- StickScope has its own logo (two sticks) as icon and in the header.
- The small windows in the main view (flight path, control schematic) can be folded away; the choice is remembered.
- The flight path is shown large, with horizon, and as a small window in the top right of the main view.
