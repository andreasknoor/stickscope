# Research dossier: Lomcevak (RC tumble, tail passes in front of the engine)

Access date for all sources: 2026-11-23. All statements are paraphrases. Direction wording is mode-independent. Note: pages were read through the fetch tool, which returns a machine summary of the page, not the raw text. Details that a summary might have distorted are flagged where relevant.

Scope warning: sources disagree on what a "lomcevak" is (forward tumble from a knife-edge climb vs. snap-based tumble vs. knife-edge spin). Each statement names its variant. The main variant with the most support is called here **forward lomcevak** (tail tumbles over the top, down elevator held, from a roughly 45 degree climb).

## 3 Phase outline (CONFIRMED statements only)

1. Setup: NOT CONFIRMED beyond "model-dependent" (S10). Numbers for throws, expo and CG are single-source (S11) and excluded here.
2. Entry: level flight, then climb at about 45 degrees (S2) with high throttle (S5).
3. Knife-edge roll: model rolled onto its side in the climb (S3).
4. Tumble start: full down elevator, full aileron and full rudder, aileron and rudder in the same direction (S4); throttle stays high (S5).
5. Tumble: several rotations, about three to four (S7). Nothing else is confirmed for this phase (no steering statement S16 confirmed).
6. Exit: NOT CONFIRMED. Only the safety rule to practise high (S8) and the existence of conflicting exit methods (S13, S14) are known.

## 4 Conflicts and open points

- C-1 Entry timing (S19): A2 gives all inputs together after the knife-edge roll; gyro gives rudder first, then aileron and elevator quickly; gyrocptr waits for the nose to drop. Not resolved.
- C-2 Throttle value (S5): about 80% (A2), full (Feral, gyrocptr, PW), medium to high or about 5/8 (gyro). "High" is confirmed, the number is not.
- C-3 Exit (S13 vs S14): neutral controls and a 45 degree down-line (A2) versus active opposite elevator/aileron/rudder (gyro). Possibly different variants, not resolved.
- C-4 Entry attitude: knife-edge roll in the climb (A2, gyrocptr) versus flipping inverted first (gyro, "inverted is better"). Variant difference.
- C-5 Rates: high rates (A2) versus low rates to look like a pro pilot's videos (Feral).
- C-6 CG: A2 says rearward; D1 poster PW says strongly tail-heavy while Steffen says normal CG (this discussion is about knife-edge spin, so applicability is uncertain).
- C-7 Left/right wording: A2 page rolls right but then calls for left rudder and aileron; not explained. Direction of rudder relative to the roll is therefore NOT CONFIRMED beyond "aileron and rudder same direction".
- C-8 Naming (S22): lomcevak vs crankshaft vs knife-edge spin.
- Variants confirmed to exist only for full-size aircraft (D2): main, cap, conic lomcovak. Not RC-verified.
- NOT FOUND: any German-language how-to; any transcript-readable video; any instructor or flight-school guide; manufacturer guidance; exact stick amounts other than "full" and "half aileron roll"; height needed (no metres given); exit throttle; a confirmed recovery procedure; a difficulty classification by two independent sources.
- Not usable due to blocking: RCU threads (the largest RC discussion source), RC Groups.

## 5 Difficulty / category remarks

- B1 (gyro): calls it difficult and similar to the crankshaft, rated medium difficulty on his scale (source-given, scale not defined). It should be tried on a simulator first, then at altitude.
- A1/A2: calls for precise inputs at the right moment and critical throttle, throws and CG.
- Evidence supports "advanced to pro" (3D aerobatics) but no two independent sources state a category. Suggested for the app: category advanced/pro, status SINGLE-SOURCE-based and unverified.
- Learning progression: NOT FOUND, except "simulator first, then high altitude" (B1) and "practise high" (A2, C1).
