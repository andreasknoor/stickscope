## 3 Phase outline from CONFIRMED statements only

1. Preparation (S17, S16, S27): CG and trim set, mixes optional; safe altitude; pass across the pilot's front (S28) at medium to high throttle (S21).
2. Entry (S1, S2, S6): quarter roll with aileron to about 90 degrees; establish the bank first, then add rudder opposite to the roll (S2). From inverted: rudder same direction as roll (S3).
3. Hold (S4, S5, S9, S10, S12, S13, S14, S22): rudder held to carry the model on its fuselage side (nose a little up); elevator corrects pitch tuck (up elevator for tuck toward gear); small aileron only if the model rolls (S15, weakly); throttle adjusts height (S22).
4. Exit (S24): quarter roll back with aileron to upright or inverted, rudder/elevator returned (R5 and R6; neutral values not individually stated, see section 7).

## 4 Hypotheses

- (a) Rudder is held toward the sky (top of the model): NOT FOUND explicitly. No source states the rudder surface direction relative to sky/ground. Related, confirmed: the nose is kept a little up and the tail lower (S5), and one source uses the term "top rudder" (R4, single). Sources instead give the cue S9 and the entry rule S2/S3. Do not present (a) as sourced.
- (b) Entry by rolling with aileron to about 90 degrees, then neutralising aileron: PARTLY CONFIRMED. The roll to about 90 degrees is confirmed (S1). Aileron held neutral afterwards is stated by R6 and consistent with R4 (aileron only as correction), so S15 is weakly confirmed. Some corrections need aileron (S14), so "neutral" is the starting value, not a guarantee.
- (c) Pitch and roll corrections are needed because of coupling: CONFIRMED (S10, S14, S16); size and direction are model dependent (S11).
- (d) Throttle is increased during the hold: PARTLY CONFIRMED. Throttle is medium to high (S21) and added when height is lost (S22). No source says throttle is increased as a rule during the hold; R4 and R1 say high or full throttle for sustained flight; R6 and R7 prefer 50-75 percent.
- (e) Direction wording mode-independent: CONFIRMED in practice. All rules for rudder relative to the aileron roll (S2, S3) and the cue S9 are mode-independent. R3 describes stick motions for one mode only and should not be used for stick positions.
- (f) Which side faces the pilot when the model flies away or across: NOT FOUND as a rule (S35). Only R3 has a single left-to-right example (belly seen after a left roll, ambiguous), and R2/R5 give the rudder cue by what the pilot sees. No source describes flying away from the pilot.

## 5 Conflicts and open points

- C1 (CONFLICT, altitude control): Rudder versus throttle. R3, R6, R7 and R9 say rudder holds height or acts like the elevator; R2 and R5 say rudder sets only the angle (nose attitude) and throttle controls altitude. The two views can both be true (rudder sets pitch/angle, throttle sets the power available), but the sources word it differently. Present only the confirmed overlap: rudder holds the attitude (S4, S5), throttle adds or removes height (S22).
- C2 (numbers differ): throttle 50-75 percent (R6), about 3/4 (R7), 1/2-3/4 (R3), full (R4, R1); rudder 1/3 (R2) versus 75 percent stick (R6); mix values vary by model. Show these only as "model dependent, source-given examples".
- C3 (roll direction under rudder): R1 says most models roll in the rudder direction (proverse); R4 says the typical tendency is to roll out of the bank (toward the rudder direction as he words it); R6 says it depends on V-form. Direction of the roll correction must be shown as model dependent.
- C4 (elevator direction for tuck): R3 and a search summary of R4 say up elevator for a tuck toward the undercarriage; my fetch of R4 says "toward the canopy or landing gear" gets up elevator. R6 gives opposite mix directions for gear versus canopy tuck. Only "up elevator for tuck toward gear" is confirmed (S12).
- C5 (difficulty category): see section 6.
- Open: no source describes the figure for a model flying directly toward or away from the pilot; no source gives a duration or distance; exit neutral inputs only partly described; stick-mode-specific descriptions (R3) were not used; R6 author to be identified; blocked RCUniverse threads (many sources) and snippet-only German pages could add independent confirmation for S25, S26, S30, S31, S33.
