## Open points
- The side to which the model swings is not given; the animation uses full left rudder and swings the nose away from the pilot (illustration note `direction`).
- No aileron in the animation (illustration note `aileron`).
- Motor gliders with a stopped or folded propeller have no propwash; only one source says what to do (gap note `propwash`).
- Minimum height: no confirmed value (gap note `min-height`).
- The up line and the down line are the same line in the air; the sketch draws them apart for legibility.
