Method limits: several pages (RCU forums, Magzter, parts of Model Airplane News) returned HTTP 403 or sit behind a paywall and were NOT read, so they are not cited. ## 3 Phase outline (CONFIRMED statements only)

1. **Preparation:** high altitude, high rates on (S10, S11). Wind direction: NOT CONFIRMED (S12 single).
2. **Entry:** reduce throttle while progressively feeding up elevator (S9), or enter from an elevator and add throttle until sinking stops (S8). Confirmed inputs: throttle down then up, elevator up.
3. **Established harrier:** nose about 45 degrees up (S2); elevator holds the attitude, throttle holds altitude by active adjustment (S1, S19); needs clearly more thrust than slow flight (S18); rudder steers heading (S3); aileron corrects bank/wing rock (S4); right rudder compensates torque/P-factor for standard prop rotation (S7).
4. **Turn:** rudder in the turn direction rolls the model the same way, opposite aileron keeps it flat (S6).
5. **Exit / recovery:** raise throttle toward full and lower the nose with elevator (S15, S16).

## 5 Conflicts and open points

- **Conflict S5 (aileron):** A (Goldberg manual) says avoid aileron in the harrier, it may snap the model. B, C and E use aileron to keep wings level and to counter wing drop. Not resolved: A is written for one high-power 3D ARF and gives no reason beyond the snap; B and C describe corrective, small inputs. The app should not show "avoid aileron" or "use aileron" as fact; show as disputed or as model-dependent until a third source decides.
- **Entry type:** B enters from level slow flight (add thrust as the model slows), E cuts throttle in level flight, A/E recommend entry from an elevator. Not contradictory, but different entries; only the elevator-derived entry (S8) is confirmed.
- **Wing-drop handling inside B:** B says add rudder to compensate if a wing drops at entry, and aileron to keep wings level in the established harrier. Both from the same author; compatible but not independently confirmed.
- **Nose angle:** 45 degrees (A, B, C) vs 30-45 degrees (E): no real conflict, range is single-source.
- **Open (no confirmation):** wind direction, speed values (km/h, m/s), exact elevator/throttle percentages beyond C's quarter-half throttle, how much aileron/rudder, harrier height/altitude loss numbers, details for electric vs glow, wing rock cause details beyond B and F, any German-language source, any source for entry speed.
- **Open:** C could only be read in part; its full text could change or add statements.

## 6 Difficulty / category remarks

- **Category: advanced beginner-3D / intermediate**, not beginner overall. Evidence: B requires prior competence in basic aerobatics (S31, single), calls the harrier a basic 3D manoeuvre needing independent use of all four controls; D calls it fundamental (quoting Chinn) and the rudder the hardest part of 3D; E labels it "Easy" in his own list; G calls it a basic 3D move teaching corrections at high AoA.
- Not a "beginner" figure in the general sense of a first RC flight. Rating "beginner within 3D" is supported by B, D, E, G (S28); the "pro" category has no source support.
- Learn in a simulator/foamie first (S27).
